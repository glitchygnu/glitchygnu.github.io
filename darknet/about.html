About Ableonion

        
Anonymous Hacker Chat & Marketplace System


        

            
> who we are

            

                Ableonion is an anonymous communication layer for people who
                value their privacy. No accounts, no emails, no phone numbers,
                no logs. You show up, you talk, you leave — nothing stays behind.
            

        


        

            
> how it works

            

                Every message is routed through an onion circuit with three hops
                (guard → middle → exit) and encrypted end-to-end with AES-256-GCM.
                The relay is a dumb pipe: it forwards ciphertext and forgets
                everything else.
            

        


        

            
> principles

            
    
                
  • no logs, no analytics, no third-party trackers
