what is ableonion

    

      Ableonion is a privacy-first communication layer built for people who
      don't want a paper trail. No accounts, no email verification, no
      phone numbers. You connect, you talk, you leave — and nothing sticks
      around after you close the tab.
    

    

      Every message rides an onion circuit. We don't know who you are, and
      we built the system so we can't know, even if someone asked us
      nicely. Or un-nicely.
    

  


  

    
principles

    
    
      
  • no logs, no analytics, no third-party trackers
    • 
      
  • no email, no phone number, no KYC — ever
    • 
      
  • end-to-end encryption by default, not as a setting
    • 
      
  • metadata minimisation: we drop timestamps and IPs on receipt
    • 
      
  • client-side everything — the server is a dumb pipe
    • 
      
  • open protocol, auditable client
    •
